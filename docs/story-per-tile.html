The Story Per Tile

What else can be joined to a 0.1 m/px segmentation raster to say how populated this ground is, what changed here, and why? Six agents went looking, over the two Middle-Eastern AOIs this project actually runs on. The answer is a great deal — but almost none of it at tile scale, and the discipline is knowing which altitude each source is allowed to speak from.

The three you asked for by name

Straight answers first

"Instagram posts from this location in the past 24 h"

No — the capability does not exist

Not gated, not paywalled, absent. IG Media exposes 29 readable fields and none is a location; Meta's docs explicitly list location, latitude and longitude as not supported. The Basic Display API has returned errors since 2024-12-04. Hashtag search does have your 24-hour window — but its axis is a hashtag, it returns no coordinates, and the quota is 30 unique hashtags per rolling week.

Every mainstream platform removed third-party geo between 2018 and 2020. X survives as a city-scale bounding box on ~1% of posts at $0.005 per read. Telegram's ToS §1.5 bans using API data for AI/ML — disqualifying here specifically.

"Traffic"

No — and it would be unusable anyway

TomTom does not support Palestine; HERE's coverage table omits it; Google and Apple disabled live traffic and busyness across Israel and Gaza in October 2023 at military request. Egypt is nominally covered by TomTom — into a depopulated restricted military zone with no probe density.

The licences close it regardless. Google's ToS §3.2.3(c)(iv) prohibits using coordinates "as an input for point-in-polygon analysis" — which is what a per-tile join is — and (c)(vii) bars using the content to train, test or validate a model.

"People per square kilometre"

Obtainable — and a confident lie in Gaza

Over one northern-Sinai window the three mainstream products return 318,868 (GHS-POP), 139,542 (WorldPop) and 114,998 (Meta HRSL). A 2.8× spread. That spread is the error bar, and no dasymetric refinement narrows it.

Palestine's census inputs are 6–16 admin units for the whole territory, so every within-governorate gradient is model output. WorldPop's Gaza series runs 63,152 (2015) → 78,469 (2024) → 87,258 (2030) — a smooth curve straight through the war.

The spine

Altitude of claim

Every source below is badged with the smallest unit it may legitimately write to. That badge is arithmetic, not opinion — it falls out of the source's pixel against this project's real units.

Read the third column and the argument is finished. WorldPop at 100 m is larger than the median Gaza block. A "people per km²" figure attached to a block is one interpolated pixel wearing a per-block label. VIIRS nightlights at 500 m and FIRMS thermal at 375 m are a quarter and a third of a tile — and the grids are not aligned, so an arbitrary tile straddles two to four of them and even the "correct" one is a blend.

  • tile

    ≤30 m. Burnable onto the raster grid, like the OSM layer. May assert about a single tile.

  • block

    ~100 m. One attribute per block or per tile-group, with the unit named in the output. Never rendered as a raster.

  • AOI

    ≥250 m. Context and alerting. May point a human or a 10 m query at a tile; may never characterise one.

  • vector

    Joins by geometry, not resolution. Precision is the record's — a building polygon is tile-precise, a news geocode is a city centroid.

The rule worth encoding: a source whose pixel is larger than a tile may only write to a spatial unit at or above its own pixel size, and a tile may only read such a value as named, attributed context. Resampling a 500 m raster to 0.5 m and reading off a tile does not create information — it launders a neighbourhood average into a claim about a courtyard.

Not asked for

Five finds worth the trip

OSM replication diffs, at 26 seconds

The honest substitute for the social-media idea, and it is already half-built here. "What did someone change on the map of this ground, 26 seconds ago" — free, legal, tile-precise. The trap: a changeset's bbox spans all its edits, and a Gaza query's top hit was a mobile changeset covering latitude 10–46°N, longitude 16–108°E. Use Overpass newer: or fetch the elements.

planet.openstreetmap.org/replication/minute/

OSM notes, in Arabic, at exact coordinates

Closer to what you were reaching for than any platform: free text pinned to a point, written by residents — منزل الو هاني الزمارة (the Zomarah family home), أرض البلدية (municipality land). 407 open notes in the Gaza City bbox. A stock, not a pulse: 405 are older than 90 days.

api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/notes.json?bbox=…

GHS-OBAT — buildings with a purpose attached

Nobody's brief mentioned it. Overture's geometry with GHSL's attributes burned on: height, area, shape factor, and use = residential / non-residential. Raw Overture carries NULL subtype on 8,401 of 8,510 Gaza buildings and zero heights; Microsoft carries zero heights across 280,081. This is the dasymetric target and the semantic layer, in one 64 MB file per country.

GHS_OBAT_GPKG_PSE_E2020_R2024A_V1_0.zip · CC BY 4.0

Your OSM tags are rich in Gaza and bare in Sinai

Gaza City: surface on 71% of ways, lanes and maxspeed on 65% — the legacy of humanitarian mapping campaigns. North Sinai: 5–6% on the same tags. So Gaza supports real capacity proxies (lane-metres, throughput) and Sinai supports only topological ones. Same code, different honest claims.

Sentinel-1 coherence, ratioed against its own baseline

Don't wait for someone's Gaza damage feed — ARIA is event-driven, the academic portal has moved to Iran and Lebanon and gates downloads, and Copernicus EMS is activation-gated. The method is public and the inputs are free. Divide by the seasonal median so you report anomaly: "expected 0.62 in autumn, today's pair reads 0.09" is defensible; a raw coherence number is not.

The bill

What each one costs you in rights

Almost everything here is free to download. Several are not free to use the way this project would use them, and one of those is a surprise.

Earth Engine is not free for you

Since 27 April 2026 the noncommercial tiers exclude for-profit entities. Commercial plans start at $500/month (Basic), with a pay-as-you-go Limited plan at $0.40 per EECU-hour. For two small AOIs the compute is trivial — the platform floor is the cost. Dynamic World's model is Apache-2.0 on GitHub, so the cheaper path is to run inference yourself on free Copernicus Sentinel-2 and never touch GEE.

Turned back on us

What the survey says about these AOIs

The most useful finding was not a data source. It was what three independent geological maps say about ground this project has been reasoning over for months.

Neither AOI contains the rocks half the taxonomy is built around. Both are Quaternary coastal plain — alluvium, kurkar, loess, dune sand. There is no marl under Gaza City and no soft carbonate under a north Sinai dune field. Then RT's own output, measured:

# classified pixels carrying a lithology-bearing class
aza    (1.07 km²)   33.5%   — of which 31.6% Marl
                     top classes: Shadow 28.9%, MaralBadlands 28.1%, House 8.9%
sinai  (174.5 km²)   3.0%
                     top classes: Rendzina 64.9%, DryGrassland 17.7%, TerraRosa 3.0%

This is the repo's own lesson arriving from outside. taxonomy.py already records that the class owner confirmed these soil labels are surface-appearance buckets, not genetic units — and four textbook priors were retired after generating 9,793 false findings on exactly that mistake. So one qualification on the otherwise sound advice to join a lithology map: it is right for the rock classes, which name a lithology. Extending it to TerraRosa/Rendzina would repeat the retired-priors error — and those are 68% of Sinai.

Three corrections to the data inventory

The "northern Sinai, Egypt" AOI is 62.5% Egypt, 25.1% open Mediterranean, 10.5% Gaza Strip and 2.0% Israel — two independent products agree on the sea fraction to 0.1%. And 70% of its buildings are in Rafah, not Egypt, so a join keyed on country silently drops two-thirds of the built environment and the geological source must switch at a border running through the AOI.

The Gaza AOI sits inside SoilGrids' coastal nodata hole — 79.7% empty — and inside HWSD2's 100%-urban polygon. Nudging it 3–4 km inland recovers both.

Limestone vs dolomite is not separable, and nari is not a map unit. The Geological Survey of Israel's own lithology derivative uses one combined class, LDM "Limestone and dolostone"; querying for NARI, CALCRETE or CALICHE returns empty at every scale, correctly — nari is a surface crust overlying mapped bedrock. UNDECIDABLE-NEEDS-geological-map is permanently unsatisfiable for nari. The map does split hard from soft carbonate, which is what actually controls those classes — so the abstain code wants renaming to NEEDS-hardness-class.

Recommendation

What to wire first

  1. Copernicus DEM GLO-30 tile

    One 29 MB tile, no auth, no non-commercial clause, no share-alike — and it closes a gap the project has documented for months. Measured height error 0.61 m over Gaza and 0.45 m over Sinai gives a slope noise floor of 1.43° and 0.69°; 97–98% of tiles clear a 3σ bar for tile-mean slope. Be equally clear about what it cannot do: per-post aspect clears 3σ on only 31–36% of posts, and dip angle never does. Drive the abstain rule from the shipped height-error mask, not a constant.

  2. GHS-BUILT-C 10 m + GHS-OBAT tile vector

    Together they answer "how populated" without ever emitting a fabricated count — 144 fabric cells inside a 125 m tile, and per-building height and residential use as vector. This reframes the question from a headcount to what kind of ground is this, which is the question a segmentation raster can honestly be joined against. Both CC BY 4.0; one file each covers all three AOIs.

  3. OSM replication stream tile

    The cheapest thing on this list and the closest to the original intent. Adds a time dimension to a join RT already performs, at 26-second latency, under a licence the project already complies with. Mind the changeset-bbox trap.

  4. Sentinel-2 + Dynamic World probabilities tile

    The only channel that both resolves a tile and is verified flowing over both AOIs. It supplies the axis the project most conspicuously lacks: your raster has one observation, this has ~83 a year. S4-products.md open question 3 already asks for exactly this — "Season is a switch, not a model." Expect it to be poor over inland Sinai; the paper names arid rangeland as its worst case.

  5. UNOSAT damage, for Gaza only vector

    Do not ship a Gaza population figure without it. WorldPop's 2024 raster grows straight through the war; UNOSAT's per-building assessment through 11 October 2025 is the only current authoritative correction, and it joins to the same footprints as pick 2. Union the damage status across all three epochs — a single-epoch read understates it badly. Licence is CC BY-SA.

And one that is not a data source. If the goal is validating the terra rossa / rendzina / nari / chalk half of the taxonomy, these are the wrong two AOIs — every source only has something to say about those classes in the Judean hills or on Carmel. A third AOI there would be worth more than any layer on this page.
